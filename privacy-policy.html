Roamance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Roamance (“Roamance”, “we”, “our” or “us”) is a travel journal for couples. You and your partner plan trips, save photo memories, pin the places you visit on a shared map and, if you choose, share stories with the Roamance community. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Roamance iOS app and related services (the “Service”), how we use it, and the choices you have.

In short: your journal is private to you and your partner by default. We never sell your personal information. Photos and location are only accessed with your permission, and you can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored only in hashed form), or the identifier and, if you choose to share it, the email address provided by Sign in with Apple; and a unique user ID (UID) we assign.
  • Profile information: your display name, date of birth, gender and profile photo. Your date of birth is used to confirm that you meet the minimum age and cannot be changed after sign-up.
  • Partner connection: invite codes you create or enter, your connection status and the date you connected with your partner.
  • Journal content: trips (destination, dates, cover photo), memories (photos, captions, mood, date and place) and anniversaries you create.
  • Community content: memories you choose to publish to Explore, and reports, blocks and “not interested” choices you make.
  • Communications: messages you send to our support team.

1.2 Information collected with your permission

  • Photos: only the photos you select are uploaded to Roamance. When you save a memory to your library we request add-only access and do not read your photo library.
  • Location: precise location while you are using the app, used to suggest a place name for a new memory and to show where you are on the map. We do not collect location in the background.
  • Notifications: a push notification token, used to tell you about your partner’s new memories, upcoming trips and anniversaries.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): only if you allow tracking through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. It is used for advertising attribution, as described below.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Declining a permission only disables the related feature.

1.3 Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone and IP address.
  • Usage data: app launches, features used, crash and performance information.
  • Attribution data: how you found and installed Roamance (for example, which advertising campaign).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and secure your account and keep you signed in;
  • Connect you with your partner and sync your shared journal between you;
  • Store and display your trips, memories, photos and map;
  • Publish the stories you choose to share in Explore and show you stories from other couples;
  • Send notifications you have allowed;
  • Review reports, enforce our Terms and community standards, and prevent fraud, spam and abuse;
  • Measure the performance of our marketing and understand how the app is used so we can improve it;
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on: performance of our contract with you (providing the Service), your consent (photos, location, notifications, tracking), our legitimate interests (security, moderation, improving the Service) and legal obligations.

3. Who Can See Your Content

  • Your partner: once you connect, your shared trips and memories are visible to your partner, and theirs to you.
  • The community: only memories you explicitly publish to Explore are visible to other users, together with your couple display name and profile photos. You can make a published story private again at any time.
  • Blocked users: people you block cannot see your public stories, and you will not see theirs.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this Policy will continue to apply.

5. Data Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. If you disconnect from your partner, shared content you created stays in your account. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, journal content and photos within 30 days, except where we must keep limited information to comply with law, resolve disputes or enforce our agreements. Reports about content may be retained for safety purposes.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and other reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and edit: update your profile and content in the app.
  • Delete your account: go to Us → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and associated data.
  • Permissions: manage photos, location, notifications and tracking in iOS Settings.
  • Other rights: depending on where you live, you may have the right to request a copy of your data, correct it, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Email support@roamance.app and we will respond within 30 days.

8. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Children

Roamance is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. If changes are significant, we will notify you in the app.
