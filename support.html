Frequently Asked Questions

1. I can’t sign in. What should I do?

Check your internet connection and make sure you use the same method you signed up with (Sign in with Apple or email). If you use email, confirm your address and password. If the problem continues, contact us with the email address on your account.

2. How do I connect with my partner?

On the Journeys tab, tap Invite my partner to get a 6-character code and share it. Your partner taps I have an invite code and enters it. Codes are single-use and expire after 24 hours; you can generate a new one at any time.

3. My partner can’t see my memories.

Make sure you are both connected (your partner appears on the Us tab) and both have the latest version of the app.

4. Why does Roamance ask for location?

Location is only used while you are using the app, to suggest a place for new memories and show where you are on the map. You can turn it off in iOS Settings → Roamance → Location; you can still add places by hand.

5. How do I report or block someone?

In Explore, tap ··· on any story and choose Report story or Block. Blocked users disappear from your feed immediately. You can unblock them in Us → Settings → Blocked users. We review every report within 24 hours.

6. How do I make a shared story private again?

In Explore, tap ··· on your story and choose Make private. It is removed from Explore right away and stays in your shared journal.

7. How do I report a bug?

Email us with your device model, iOS version, app version (Us → Settings), a description of the issue and screenshots if possible.

8. How do I delete my account?

Go to Us → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, trips, memories and photos. You can also email us from the address linked to your account and we will process the request.

9. The app isn’t working properly.

  • Close and reopen the app
  • Update to the latest version from the App Store
  • Restart your device

If the problem continues, please contact us.
